03 — Operating Principles

How I think.

01

North-star over vanity

Every roadmap item must trace to one measurable user or business outcome. No exceptions, no decorative work.

02

Platforms compound

Build reusable primitives. One good API replaces ten one-off features. Leverage beats heroics every quarter.

03

Data is a teammate

SQL fluency, RFM, cohort thinking, and instrumentation-first design. If it isn’t measured, it didn’t ship.

04

Speed with taste

Ship weekly, iterate fearlessly, but never at the cost of craft. Polish is a competitive moat.
